About the Club

Every student has a role.

CCHS Drama Club is the official drama club of Cleveland Central High School in Cleveland, Mississippi.

We help students build confidence, creativity, teamwork, leadership, and communication skills through theatre arts and stage production.

01

Perform

Develop stage presence, confidence, expression, and strong communication skills.

02

Create

Explore acting, costumes, props, set design, lighting, sound, and storytelling.

03

Collaborate

Work as one cast and crew to turn an idea into a complete live production.
