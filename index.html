About Us

Confidence. Creativity. Teamwork.

CCHS Drama Club is the official drama club of Cleveland Central High School in Cleveland, Mississippi. We strive to build confidence, foster creativity, and cultivate teamwork through theatre arts and stage production.

Whether performing under the lights, designing sets, working backstage, or supporting fellow cast members, every student has an opportunity to grow, develop leadership skills, and become part of a welcoming theatre family.

01

Perform

Develop confidence, stage presence, and communication skills.

02

Create

Explore acting, design, costumes, props, lighting, sound, and technical theatre.

03

Collaborate

Work together as a cast and crew to bring each production to life.
