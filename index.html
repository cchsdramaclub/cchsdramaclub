About Us

More than a club. We are a company.

The CCHS Drama Club gives students a place to explore acting, directing, technical theatre, costume design, set construction, lighting, sound, stage management, and more.

Whether you love being in the spotlight or prefer working behind the scenes, there is a place for you in the Wolfpack Drama Club.

01

Perform

Build confidence, develop characters, and tell meaningful stories on stage.

02

Create

Help design sets, costumes, props, lighting, sound, and publicity.

03

Belong

Join a supportive community of students who share a love for theatre.
