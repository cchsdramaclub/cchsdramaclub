About the Club

Confidence.
Creativity.
Teamwork.

CCHS Drama Club is the official drama club of Cleveland Central High School in Cleveland, Mississippi.

We strive to build confidence, foster creativity, and cultivate teamwork through theatre arts and stage production.

Whether performing under the lights, designing sets, working backstage, or supporting fellow cast members, every student has an opportunity to grow, lead, and become part of a welcoming theatre family.

01

Perform

Build confidence, stage presence, and strong communication skills.

02

Explore acting, design, costumes, props, sound, lighting, and more.

03

Work as one cast and crew to bring every production to life.
